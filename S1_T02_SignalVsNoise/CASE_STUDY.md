# Case-study brief — uncertainty in an MND trial

- **Contributor inspiration:** Suvankar Pal; MND-SMART, CARE-MND and neurodegenerative clinical trials.
- **Clinical question:** Is the observed difference in functional decline compatible with a useful treatment effect, or with sampling variation?
- **Unit of observation:** one fictional trial participant.
- **Teaching move:** estimate a group difference, confidence interval and p-value; resample to show instability.
- **Designed feature:** the active arm has a modest average benefit with substantial person-to-person variation and incomplete follow-up.
- **Misconception to surface:** a non-significant result is not evidence of identical effects.
- **Do not claim:** the fictional active treatment or effect represents an MND-SMART arm.
- **Contributor confirmation:** acceptable functional-score language and a clinically meaningful difference.

> This is a synthetic teaching dataset containing no real participant records. Its values and results are fictional.
