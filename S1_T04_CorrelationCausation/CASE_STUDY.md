# Case-study brief — causal claims from real-world treatment data

- **Contributor inspiration:** Sjoerd Beentjes; causal questions, targeted learning and real-world evidence.
- **Clinical question:** Does an observed treatment–outcome association estimate what would happen under treatment?
- **Unit of observation:** one fictional patient eligible for treatment.
- **Teaching move:** compare crude outcomes, stratify by severity, draw a causal diagram, and define a target trial.
- **Designed feature:** treatment is genuinely beneficial in the generator but is preferentially given to sicker patients, making its crude outcome look harmful.
- **Misconception to surface:** precision from a large sample does not remove confounding bias.
- **Do not claim:** adjustment in the classroom analysis identifies a real causal effect.
- **Contributor confirmation:** causal structure, target estimand and acceptable terminology.

> This is a synthetic teaching dataset containing no real patient records. Its values and results are fictional.
