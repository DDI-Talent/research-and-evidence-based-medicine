# Case-study brief — describing a critical-care cohort

- **Contributor inspiration:** Nazir Lone; critical-care epidemiology, linked data and multimorbidity.
- **Clinical question:** Who is represented in an ICU admissions cohort, and why is the “average ICU patient” potentially misleading?
- **Unit of observation:** one fictional ICU admission.
- **Teaching move:** classify variables, describe missingness, pair centre with spread, and visualise skew and unusual values.
- **Designed feature:** length of stay is right-skewed; severity and organ support are related to outcome; small amounts of outcome-related data are missing.
- **Misconception to surface:** extreme stays are not automatically errors, and distributional position does not define clinical abnormality.
- **Do not claim:** these distributions describe Scottish ICUs or Professor Lone's studies.
- **Contributor confirmation:** terminology, plausible ranges, and the most useful opening clinical question.

> This is a synthetic teaching dataset containing no real patient records. Its values and results are fictional.
