# Case-study brief — population evidence and perinatal decisions

- **Contributor inspiration:** Clara Calvert; population reproductive, maternal and child health using routine and observational data.
- **Clinical question:** Which pregnancies should be offered additional preventive support, and what trade-offs follow from the threshold?
- **Unit of observation:** one fictional pregnancy.
- **Teaching move:** compare regional baseline risks, apply thresholds, count benefits and burdens, and test decisions under uncertainty.
- **Designed feature:** screening access and baseline risk vary by region and deprivation; support reduces generated risk but introduces burden.
- **Misconception to surface:** the highest-risk group is not necessarily where an intervention produces the greatest population benefit if access and uptake differ.
- **Do not claim:** the screening programme, intervention or regional differences exist in Scotland or represent Dr Calvert's findings.
- **Contributor confirmation:** decision context, equity framing, plausible benefits and burdens.

> This is a synthetic teaching dataset containing no real pregnancy records. Its values and results are fictional.
