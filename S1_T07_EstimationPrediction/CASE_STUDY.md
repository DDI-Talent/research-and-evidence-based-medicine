# Case-study brief — colorectal-cancer prediction

- **Contributor inspiration:** Nathalie Feeley; colorectal cancer genomics, tumour evolution and APC inactivation.
- **Clinical question:** Can baseline clinical and simplified genomic features predict two-year progression in new patients?
- **Unit of observation:** one fictional colorectal tumour/patient record.
- **Teaching move:** train a logistic model, generate probabilities, and compare development with external-validation performance, discrimination and calibration.
- **Designed feature:** stage is strongly predictive; molecular features add signal; a pre-labelled external-validation cohort prevents accidental evaluation on training data.
- **Misconception to surface:** good model fit or biological interest does not guarantee useful prediction or mechanism.
- **Do not claim:** the simplified APC categories reproduce real genotypes, effect sizes or Professor Feeley's results.
- **Contributor confirmation:** molecular terminology, acceptable simplification and clinically relevant prediction target.

> This is a synthetic teaching dataset containing no real tumour or patient records. Its values and results are fictional.
