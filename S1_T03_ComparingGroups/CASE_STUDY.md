# Case-study brief — comparing islet-transplant outcomes

- **Contributor inspiration:** Shareen Forbes; islet transplantation and assessment of graft function.
- **Clinical question:** Do graft-function outcomes differ across fictional transplant strategies, and are the differences clinically meaningful?
- **Unit of observation:** one fictional transplant recipient at 12 months.
- **Teaching move:** compare two or three groups using plots, differences in means, ANOVA and proportions.
- **Designed feature:** groups differ moderately in a fictional graft-function score and insulin independence.
- **Misconception to surface:** an overall ANOVA result neither identifies the differing groups nor establishes clinical importance.
- **Do not claim:** `graft_function_score` is BETA-2 or a validated clinical instrument.
- **Contributor confirmation:** plausible variables, group labels and meaningful effect size.

> This is a synthetic teaching dataset containing no real recipient records. Its values and results are fictional.
