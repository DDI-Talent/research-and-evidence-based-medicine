# Case-study brief — risk in a longitudinal child-development study

- **Contributor inspiration:** Bonnie Auyeung; CHILDS and prenatal influences on child development.
- **Clinical question:** How does the observed probability of needing developmental support differ by a recorded prenatal exposure?
- **Unit of observation:** one fictional mother–child pair.
- **Teaching move:** calculate absolute risk, risk difference, relative risk and odds ratio with a stated follow-up period.
- **Designed feature:** exposure, deprivation, preterm birth, outcome and follow-up are related; some children are not observed at follow-up.
- **Misconception to surface:** an observed risk ratio is not automatically a causal effect, and odds are not risks.
- **Do not claim:** a particular exposure causes developmental difficulty or that the values are CHILDS findings.
- **Contributor confirmation:** exposure wording, ethical framing and preferred outcome description.

> This is a synthetic teaching dataset containing no real child or parent records. Its values and results are fictional.
