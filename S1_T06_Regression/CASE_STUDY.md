# Case-study brief — regression and ME biomarkers

- **Contributor inspiration:** Chris Ponting; DecodeME, disease genomics and ME biomarker research.
- **Clinical question:** Is a fictional biomarker associated with ME after accounting for measured competing explanations?
- **Unit of observation:** one fictional study participant.
- **Teaching move:** fit crude and adjusted models and interpret coefficients in plain clinical language.
- **Designed feature:** biomarker concentration differs by ME status and activity; activity is also strongly related to ME status.
- **Misconception to surface:** adjustment does not prove causality, and controlling for a consequence of illness may answer a different question.
- **Do not claim:** the biomarker exists, diagnoses ME, causes ME, or represents a DecodeME finding.
- **Contributor confirmation:** respectful terminology, plausible causal diagram and model sequence.

> This is a synthetic teaching dataset containing no real participant records. Its values and results are fictional.
